# Sentinel — Pitch Video Script

**Duration:** Under2 minutes
**Format:** You on camera, talking directly to the viewer
**Tone:** Confident, direct, no fluff

---

## Script (60-90 seconds)

**[Look at camera]**

> "Hi, I'm Ubong. I built Sentinel — on-chain guardrails for AI agents on Monad."

**[The problem]**

> "AI agents are moving real money now. Trading, swapping, lending — all without human review. If an agent gets compromised or makes a bad decision, your wallet is drained in one transaction. There's no undo button on-chain."

**[The solution]**

> "Sentinel solves this at the smart contract level. Every agent transaction passes through on-chain checks: spending limits, address whitelists, time-locks, and circuit breaks. If a transaction violates any rule, it's reverted before it executes."

**[The killer feature]**

> "Here's what makes Sentinel different. Using EIP-7702, the agent's wallet literally becomes the guardrail contract. It can't bypass the rules even if it tries — because the rules ARE the wallet. On-chain, enforced, unbypassable."

**[Proof]**

> "I built this solo for Monad Metropolis. Three contracts deployed on Monad Testnet. A working product with policy creation, agent registration, and real-time activity monitoring. It's live right now."

**[Close]**

> "Sentinel. Agents that can't go rogue. Thank you."

---

## Recording tips
- Stand or sit upright, look at the camera lens
- Good lighting (face the window or a lamp)
- Speak clearly, not too fast
- Background: clean wall or your workspace
- Keep it under 2 minutes — the script above is about 60-90 seconds
- One take is fine, don't overthink it
